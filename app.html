📦 MsgPackLens

Paste MessagePack bytes (hex or base64). See the decoded value as a tree, every byte accounted for, hover to connect tree nodes to their exact bytes. Full-spec decoder, verified against the real msgpack library. About

Everything decodes in your browser - nothing is uploaded. Presets are encoded in-page by the same engine, then decoded. Tested against the real msgpack library for values and canonical bytes, plus an independent span walker for byte ranges (tests/).
